Qibla AR Finder

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Qibla AR Finder uses location, camera, and compass data to help users find the Qibla direction and view the AR overlay safely.

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Information We Use

Qibla AR Finder uses only the information needed to provide its core features on your device.

Location

  • The app requests location access to calculate Qibla direction and local prayer times.
  • Location is used on-device to support app functionality.
  • The last known location may be stored locally to support offline-friendly behavior.

Camera

  • The app requests camera access only for the AR overlay mode.
  • Camera imagery is used live on-device and is not uploaded by the app.

Sensor Data

  • The app uses the device compass and related sensors to determine heading.
  • Sensor readings are processed in real time on-device and are not sent to a backend service.

Data Storage

  • The app stores cached location and prayer schedule data locally on the device.
  • The app does not require a user account.
  • The app does not operate a backend service to collect personal data from users.

Data Sharing

  • The app does not sell personal data.
  • The app does not share location, camera, or sensor data with third parties for advertising.

Security

  • Cached app data is stored locally on your device.
  • You can clear the app data through Android settings to remove local cache.

Children's Privacy

Qibla AR Finder is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect children's personal data through a server-based backend.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes or distribution requirements.
